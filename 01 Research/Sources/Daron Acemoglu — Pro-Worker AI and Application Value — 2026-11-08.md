## Executive decision

**Capture an economic/product-discovery distinction; do not build a new harness component.** The interview argues that the largest opportunities are domain applications and high-quality data informed by practitioners, not simply stronger foundation models or replacing workers. This is a thesis, not demonstrated sector-level investment returns. The valuable HE refinement is: **local task speed, newly affordable automated analysis, and genuinely new human capabilities are distinct outcomes**.

## Primary-research cross-check

1. The [2024 NBER paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32487) forecasts *no more than about 0.66% extra total factor productivity over 10 years* under its task-cost assumptions. GDP and TFP are not the same measure; observed GDP is not established by this model.
2. The [February 2026 NBER working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34854) distinguishes **labor augmentation, capital augmentation, automation, expertise leveling and new human task creation**. The authors call only *new human task creation* **unambiguously pro-worker** in their framework. This is an economic/labor-demand classification, not a moral rating of a specific feature.
3. The [2025 QJE study](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658) reported **15% more issues resolved per hour across 5,172 customer service agents**, with heterogeneous effects. It is direct workflow evidence, not economy-wide proof.
4. The [March 2026 firm survey](https://bfi.uchicago.edu/working-papers/firm-data-on-ai/) covers nearly **6,000 senior executives** in the US/UK/Germany/Australia; approximately **9 in 10 reported no employment or productivity impact over the prior three years**, while expectations of future gains were positive. This is self-report at firm level, not a controlled experiment.
5. These two empirical results can coexist: measurable gains in a narrow task do not automatically aggregate into a company-wide result after deployment, training, workflow integration, quality, staffing and demand.

## Distinct opportunity classes

- **A. Existing task gets cheaper/faster:** e.g. an AI-produced CI incident summary. Outcome requires *correctness*, not just speed.
- **B. Previously uneconomic work becomes feasible:** e.g. broad cross-repo drift checks that were never done manually. **HE already covers this** in `Austin Marchese — Anthropic Growth Workflows, Sub-Threshold Work & Bespoke Tools — 2026-09-29.md`. It may be fully automated; it is **not necessarily pro-worker** in the 2026 paper's sense.
- **C. Workers can perform genuinely new valuable tasks:** e.g. engineers use validated evidence and tools to perform new classes of diagnosis, coordination and review. This is the distinctive economic idea; first prove the capability and demand rather than asserting labor benefits.

**Conditional HE product-discovery prompts** (use in an existing decision process, no new required form):
- Which real practitioner is blocked, and what is the observed limitation?
- What actually changes for that practitioner? Existing-work speed, previously impossible analysis, or new work they will perform?
- What evidence would show net value after verification, integration, maintenance, trust/permissions and opportunity costs?

**For PMB:** context capture is not automatically product value; look for correct recall, fewer erroneous promotions, and decisions that genuinely improve. **For ACR:** more findings is not the goal; confirmed useful findings, false-positive burden, and fix verification matter. **For HE:** fewer human keystrokes is not equivalent to better release outcomes.

## Qualifications

- The economics paper is a working paper/framework; not every business will share its labor-market assumptions.
- Better model demos can be real and useful even if macro gains lag. Conversely, a financial forecast about data/application value is not a business plan for a particular project.
- User experience, employer compliance, regulated occupations, privacy, worker compensation and data rights are constraints on application success.
- An interview's recommendation to talk to workers is not permission to ingest private chats or work histories into a memory system.
- **No installed tools, workflow changes, AI council, ROI dashboard or product requirement follow from this source.**
