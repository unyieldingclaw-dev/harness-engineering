## Research-use note

The repository is the evidence source for the findings in the accompanying deep evidence pass. Conclusions about PMB are explicitly analysis/disposition, not claims made by the Hindsight project.

## Important source claims

- Memory is filtered for future behavioral value rather than recorded as a session journal.
- Root-cause compression is preferred over recording surface symptoms.
- Concrete session claims are checked against what actually happened.
- Broader lessons are separately classified as proved or suggested.
- Existing memory topics are updated in place rather than duplicated.
- Persistent-memory mutation is user-triggered rather than model-invoked.
- The skill does not modify project code/content as a side effect.
