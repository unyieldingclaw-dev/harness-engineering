## Executive interpretation

**METR's audit provides substantial evidence of a faster improvement trend on Anthropic's internal capabilities benchmark. It does *not* prove fully autonomous AI R&D, a product release, fully reproducible public evaluation, or universal 2x business/engineering productivity.**

Separate four statements:

1. **Benchmark capability:** score trends on Anthropic’s internal AECI measure for relatively well-specified tasks.
2. **Model-development speed:** how rapidly capability improves over calendar time (affected by AI labor, compute, staffing, data and other mechanisms).
3. **Human-researcher throughput:** staff reports of uplift and selective observational data; self-report has selection and estimation error.
4. **Autonomous science/R&D:** independently originating, executing and verifying a consequential research advance with minimal human support.

No one measurement proves the other three.

## HE relevance

The source strengthens existing *measurement semantics and independent acceptance* rather than supplying a new architecture:

- **Causality:** distinguish “a metric went up” from “our new harness/agent caused it.” Examine model upgrades, data, task distribution, longer budgets, human support and environment.
- **Task horizon:** well-specified benchmark progress is useful but may not capture messy cross-system outcomes, user intent, safety, long-run maintenance or organizational diffusion.
- **Authority:** keep acceptance criteria and policy ownership separate from the producing model. Independent external reviewers still need enough disclosed source/method details to reproduce conclusions.
- **Evidence visibility:** treat undisclosed methodology as **unverified by the public**, not as proof of misconduct or a license to assert hidden capabilities.
- **Evaluation records:** source/version/timestamp, measurement window, task distribution, tools, model/effort, budget and confidence/uncertainty should be inspectable when relevant to a real HE evaluation.
- **No “agent count = autonomy” inference:** large numbers of agents or costly inference do not establish validated independent research contributions.
